This dataset is prepared for regulatory research, export-control analysis, historical studies, educational use, and non-production software testing.

It is not a live restricted-party screening or export-compliance determination service.

## 2. Official Regulatory Source

The dataset references the BIS Entity List, published in Supplement No. 4 to Part 744 of the Export Administration Regulations (EAR).

Official reference:

## 3. Data Currency

BIS Entity List entries, addresses, license requirements, and related regulatory provisions may change.

Records may be added, amended, or removed after the dataset's snapshot date.

Users must verify current requirements against official BIS publications and applicable regulations.

## 4. Regulatory Interpretation

- Inclusion in the dataset does not independently establish the legal status of a particular transaction.
- Absence from the dataset does not establish that a transaction is permitted.
- License requirements depend on the applicable regulations, listed entry, transaction, and controlled items.
- Other export-control restrictions may apply independently of the Entity List.

Users remain responsible for appropriate regulatory due diligence.
